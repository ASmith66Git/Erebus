1. Fix the characteristic write type
In your current code, you have a comment suggesting the use of writeCharacteristicWithoutResponseForService. However, for the 0x35 handshake, Shearwater's UDS protocol generally expects a confirmed write (withResponse).

Issue: Using "without response" for a critical security handshake can lead to packet loss or the device ignoring the command entirely, causing the app to wait indefinitely for an ACK until it times out.

Fix: Ensure your writeCharacteristic call for the 0x35 command uses withResponse: true.

2. Add "GATT Warm-up" Delays
Your code already has some delays, but they may be too short for the initial handshake on v93 firmware. Shearwater devices often need a significant pause after service discovery before they are ready to process a UDS session request.

Adjustment: Increase the delay after discoverAllServicesAndCharacteristics() to at least 2000ms before sending the 0x35 request.

Why: This gives the Perdix's internal BLE stack time to fully enumerate the GATT table and "unlock" the security layer required for UDS.

3. Handle the "Bonding" Requirement
For iOS 17+ (and your log's iOS 26.3 build), secure handshakes like 0x35 often require the device to be paired/bonded at the OS level.

Symptom: If the device isn't bonded, iOS may silently drop the handshake response for security reasons, resulting in your Packet receive timeout.

Fix:

Go to your iPhone Bluetooth Settings and ensure the Perdix is explicitly paired.

In your code, ensure you are not calling cancelDeviceConnection too aggressively between retries, as this can break the bonding session.
