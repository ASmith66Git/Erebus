Recommended Strategy for Honor Magic V3 + Android 15
A. Increase Discovery Timeout
Android 15 discovery can be sluggish on foldables like the V3 due to the way the antenna handles the 2.4GHz band while folded/unfolded.

Fix: If you are using react-native-ble-plx, do not call discoverAllServicesAndCharacteristics() immediately. Wrap it in a setTimeout of at least 3000ms after the connect() promise resolves.

B. Handle the "Service Not Found" Logic
The log says: "BLE: Shearwater service not found, disconnecting to clear GATT cache". If your code manually disconnects when it doesn't see the service, you might be trapped in a loop.

Fix: Instead of disconnecting immediately, try to read the RSSI or request an MTU change first. This "pings" the device and often forces the GATT table to refresh without a full teardown.

C. Database Synchronization
The NativeDatabase error suggests your app is trying to "get last sync time" while the BLE connection is failing.

Fix: Ensure your database prepare statements are inside a try/catch and that you aren't calling them until the BLE device object is fully verified and "Ready."
