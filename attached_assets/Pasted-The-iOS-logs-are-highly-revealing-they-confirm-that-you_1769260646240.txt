2. Why the Perdix is Silent
There are three likely reasons why the Perdix is ignoring your 0x22 0x8020 command:

Session Initialization: In UDS (Unified Diagnostic Services), you often cannot jump straight to a "Read Data" (0x22) command. The device may require a Diagnostic Session Control or Download Init (0x35) command first to "unlock" the data registers.

Incorrect DID: The identifier 0x8020 might not be valid for this specific firmware version or might be protected. If the device doesn't recognize the ID, it may send a Negative Response (0x7F) or simply ignore the request if the session isn't active.

"Remembers" Previous Device: Some Shearwater computers "remember" the last bonded device (like your phone's official app) and may refuse to talk to a custom app if it thinks the "authorized" session hasn't properly closed.
