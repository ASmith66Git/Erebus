The Diagnostic Smoking Gun: In your logs, the app reports it is trying to write to characteristic ...9bd2. However, according to your code, 9bd2 is a Service UUID, not a Write Characteristic UUID.

You are effectively telling the Perdix: "I want to write data to the folder itself" instead of "I want to write data to the file inside the folder." iOS sees you trying to perform a "Write" operation on a "Service" (which is a container, not a writable endpoint) and immediately kills the connection with "Failed to connect to device".
